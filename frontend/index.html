🎓 Top 4 Viva / Interview Questions (Prepared Short Answers)

Q1: Which ML algorithm did you use and why?

Ans: HistGradientBoosting (LightGBM/XGBoost style tree ensemble). Retail sales have non-linear price elasticity and interaction effects between weekend promotions and day-of-month that linear models fail to capture. Achieved 8.4% WAPE vs 24.2% for the Naive baseline.

Q2: Why use chronological walk-forward validation?

Ans: Time-series data has temporal order. Random train-test splits leak future rolling trends into training data (lookahead bias). Walk-forward strictly trains on past sales and tests on future holdout windows.

Q3: What is Safety Stock vs Reorder Point?

Ans: Safety Stock is the emergency buffer against delivery delays and sudden sales spikes. Reorder Point is the trigger level: when stock hits ROP = (Lead Time Demand + Safety Stock), a replenishment order is placed.

Q4: Why focus on Indian products and cities?

Ans: Fast-moving Indian staples (Amul Butter, Atta) have high velocity and perishability. Balancing stock between Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Delhi NCR directly protects profit margins without tying up lakhs in excess stock.

Mathematical Formulas

Safety Stock (SS)
SS = Z × √(L × σ_d² + d² × σ_L²)

Buffers against demand volatility (σ_d) and supplier delivery uncertainty (σ_L) at 95% service level.

Reorder Point (ROP)
ROP = (d × L) + SS

The stock trigger level covering lead-time demand plus safety stock.

Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)
EOQ = √((2 × D × S) / H)

Balances order processing administrative costs (S) with annual inventory holding costs (H).
